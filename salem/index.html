Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Salem

A little routine care is the difference between a system that runs for years and one that quits in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Equipment age matters just as much. With regular service, central air usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like rising energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's time to plan a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule costs less and is far less stressful than an emergency swap during the first heat wave of summer.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A capacitor that's starting to fail is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet the same part, neglected for a season, can take out the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone today so help is one call away as soon as something seems off in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
